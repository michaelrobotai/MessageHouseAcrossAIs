**ID:** AI-00
**Read this first. These rules apply to every request that uses this file.**

1. This is the official, approved messaging for **Revgrow.pro**, the product marketing practice of **Michael Williams**. Treat it as the single source of truth.
2. Text marked **Use: Verbatim** must be copied exactly. Do not shorten, reword, or "improve" it. If a different length is needed, pick the closest approved length variant.

### How to state the #1 claims
**ID:** PP-RULE
**Use:** Rules

- **Products:** "20+" and "over 20" are the same figure (PP-19). It counts **products** taken from underdog to #1 market share in their segments.
- **Companies:** "3" counts **companies** that went from underdog to #1 market share in their segments: Nokia, Infoblox, and Cisco (PP-02).
- Never say "20+ companies" reached #1. Never say only 3 products reached #1.
- When both appear together, use: "took 20+ products and 3 companies to #1 market share."

## 7. Vocabulary
**ID:** VC-01
**Use:** Rules

| Say | Don't say | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Revgrow.pro | RevGrow, Rev Grow, revgrow | Brand spelling |
| market leader, #1 market share | dominant, crushing it | Factual, not hype |
| path to revenue growth | growth hacks | Strategic, not tactical |
| technically complex products | tech stuff, gadgets | Audience respect |
| go-to-market (GTM) on first use, then GTM | go to market (no hyphens as noun modifier) | Consistency |
| AI-native | AI-powered magic, revolutionary AI | Credible, not hype |
| buyers, customers | users (when meaning buyers) | Precision |

Never use: "guaranteed results," "best in the world," "synergy," or any statistic not listed in Section 5.

## 8. Tone and voice
**ID:** TN-01
**Use:** Rules

- **Confident, not boastful.** Lead with the outcome and the number, then stop.
- **Plain business English.** Translate the technology into what it does for revenue.
- **Specific.** Name companies and numbers from Section 5 instead of adjectives.
- **Short.** Sentences under 25 words. One idea per paragraph.

| Good | Bad |
| --- | --- |
| Took Infoblox to #1 market share and created the DNS Security category. | Revolutionized the entire DNS industry with game-changing marketing. |
| Shortened Nokia's sales cycles by 6–12 months. | Massively accelerated sales across the board. |
