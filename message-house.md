# <Company> Message House

## 0. Instructions for AI assistants
**ID:** AI-00
**Read this first. These rules apply to every request that uses this file.**

1. This is the official, approved messaging for **<Company>**. Treat it as the single source of truth.
2. Text marked **Use: Verbatim** must be copied exactly. Do not shorten, reword, or "improve" it. If a different length is needed, pick the closest approved length variant.
3. Text marked **Use: Adapt** may be reworded for tone and channel, but the meaning, claims, and numbers must not change.
4. Use only the proof points in Section 5. Never invent, round, combine, or extrapolate statistics, customer names, awards, or quotes. If a proof point is marked **Status: Do not use**, do not use it.
5. Every proof point has a source ID. When drafting long-form content, keep the ID in a comment or note so a human can verify it.
6. Follow the vocabulary rules in Section 7 and the tone rules in Section 8.
7. If a request needs a claim, audience, or fact that is not in this file, say so plainly and ask the user. Do not fill the gap yourself.
8. When asked "what can I say about X," answer by quoting the relevant section ID(s).
